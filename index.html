Erra P.

Techno & House · Ireland / Amsterdam · since 2016

Erra P. is Steffen Coonan (AiSenma) and Ciaran O’Brien (Kiki Kuuki), a duo of artists, music producers, and DJs based in Ireland and Amsterdam, known for their confident and refined approach to Techno and House music. Their sound is unapologetically dance-floor focused and rooted in the underground, with a minimal, reductive approach to production and mixing that keeps every track clear, mature and hypnotic.
